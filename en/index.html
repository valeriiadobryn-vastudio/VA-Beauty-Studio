Beauty salon in Palma (Ponent)

Manicure, pedicure, technical pedicure, nails and facials in Ponent.

Read more

VA Beauty Studio is a beauty salon in the Ponent district of Palma de Mallorca, at Carrer Albó 2. We offer manicures and pedicures with gel polish or gel, pain-free technical pedicure (for men too), nail extensions and facial treatments such as Hydrofacial, microneedling and peels. We work with sterilised instruments and quality products, and we speak English, Spanish, German, Ukrainian and Russian. Book online or on WhatsApp, and we'll confirm your day and time personally.

Questions and answers

How do I book an appointment?

Use the form on this website, message us on WhatsApp or call +34 684 301 949. We confirm every appointment personally.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 18:00. Closed on Sunday.

Where is the salon?

Carrer Albó 2, local 3, Ponent, 07013 Palma (Mallorca).

Which languages do you speak?

English, Spanish, German, Ukrainian and Russian.

How much do treatments cost?

Manicure from €35, pedicure from €55, nail extensions from €70, additional services from €5 and facial treatments from €55. The exact price depends on the treatment.

Do you offer technical pedicure?

Yes. We offer technical pedicure (Pedicura técnica): pain-free nail and foot care from €25. A full technical pedicure is €55. Message us on WhatsApp to choose a time.
