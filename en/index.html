Questions and answers

How do I book an appointment?

Use the form on this website, message us on WhatsApp or call +34 684 301 949. We confirm every appointment personally.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 18:00. Closed on Sunday.

Where is the salon?

Carrer Albó 2, local 3, Ponent, 07013 Palma (Mallorca).

Which languages do you speak?

English, Spanish, German, Ukrainian and Russian.

How much do treatments cost?

Manicure from €30, pedicure from €50, nail extensions from €5 and facial treatments from €55. The exact price depends on the treatment.

Do you offer technical pedicure?

Yes. We offer technical pedicure (Pedicura técnica): pain-free nail and foot care. Message us on WhatsApp to choose a time.
